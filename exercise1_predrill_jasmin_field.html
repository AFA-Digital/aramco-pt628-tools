PT-628 · Day 1 · Activity 5.1

Jasmin Field — Planning the Acquisition Programme

Group exercise · Case study: two contrasting wells, one logging programme decision each

Scenario briefing

Two new wells are being planned in Jasmin Field. Before either spuds, your team needs to design the borehole image acquisition programme for each — conveyance method and tool type — weighing the geological objective against the drilling and mud constraints each well presents. Get this wrong and you either can't run the tool at all, or run one that can't answer the question it was acquired for.

Your asset team

This is where your Frontier Exploration Asset Team comes together for the first time. Split these five roles across your table — you'll carry them through the rest of the course's group exercises as the same wells get drilled and interpreted.

PetrophysicistSpecifies which measurements and tool types will answer the objective.
Drilling ManagerFlags what conveyance is actually feasible given mud type and trajectory.
Geologist/GeophysicistDefines the geological objective driving the programme.
Exploration ManagerWeighs programme cost and risk against the project's goals.
GeosteererConsiders what real-time data the well will need while drilling.

Step 1 — Jasmin-1 (exploration appraisal well) Petrophysicist's call, with Drilling Manager's constraints

TrajectoryVertical (max 5° inclination)
Mud systemWater-based mud (WBM)
Primary objectiveStructural dip determination and natural fracture identification for exploration risking
Programme constraintCost-conscious — this is a appraisal well, not a producer

Step 2 — Jasmin-6 (high-angle producer) Petrophysicist's call, with Drilling Manager's constraints

TrajectoryHigh-angle, 68° inclination through the reservoir section
Mud systemOil-based mud (OBM) — required for shale section stability
Primary objectiveReal-time geosteering and fracture-swarm avoidance while drilling
Programme constraintConventional wireline conveyance is unreliable above ~60–65° without additional tractoring

Step 3 — Present the programme Exploration Manager presents

Summarise both wells' acquisition programmes and be ready to justify the contrast between them to the group.
